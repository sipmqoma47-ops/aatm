Our Story

Building relationships and delivering excellence since 2018

We are an industrial Wholesale and Supply Company based in 30194 Mashiyane Street, Tsakane, Brakpan. We have been servicing the engineering and industrial sector since 2018.

We have a young dynamic management and sales team waiting to assist you with all your tooling requirements and dedicated drivers will always ensure the quickest delivery possible.

AV Magagula Industrial Supplier supplies the Mining & Transport engineering and industrial sector with a comprehensive range of quality products.

We lead the way in today's competitive tooling market by offering our clients outstanding service combined with competitive prices. As a Level 1 B-BBEE Contributor, we are dedicated to providing a quality range of greases, lubricants, and cleaning materials to the automotive, mining, engineering, agricultural and commercial markets.
