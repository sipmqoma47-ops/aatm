OUR STORY

Powering South African Industry

Amathombeni Avukile Black Mineral and Projects (AABMP) is a trusted supplier of premium industrial lubricants and supplies, serving the mining, automotive, and heavy machinery sectors across South Africa.

Founded on the principles of quality, reliability, and exceptional service, we have built our reputation by consistently delivering products that meet the demanding requirements of industrial operations.

Our comprehensive product range includes hydraulic oils, engine oils, gear oils, grease, degreasers, hand cleaners, and paraffin from leading manufacturers. We understand that equipment downtime costs money, which is why we ensure fast, reliable delivery and technical support to keep your operations running smoothly.

At AABMP, we don't just supply products—we partner with our clients to provide solutions that optimize performance, reduce costs, and extend equipment life.
